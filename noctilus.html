This anthology was born from a pact between the author and the Blessed Virgin Mary. It brings together previously unpublished stories, along with others that first appeared in various Spanish-language magazines and fanzines, now translated into English for the first time.

NOCTILUS: Life and Oneiric Works of Eric Krause is the work of a fragment of the author that is no longer with us: the late Eric Teobaldo Krause, as the author himself calls him. Through these short stories, readers will be invited into Eric's unconscious, where they will encounter his thoughts, fears, passions, and, above all, his dreams.
